# Ember Tales

Ember Tales is a local storytelling addon for World of Warcraft: Forever.
Its forty stories are written in English for a long-lived kaldorei druid who has
listened to people on both sides of a war.

## At the fire

1. Place the `EmberTales` folder directly in `_forever_/Interface/AddOns/`.
   Enable Ember Tales in the AddOns list and enter the world or run `/reload`.
2. Open `/tale`. Choose a **Story** and a **Point of view**. **I was there**
   assumes the druid was present. **I heard a tale** tells someone else's
   account. **I put it together** follows the clues he noticed and the
   questions they left him with.
   The **Road adventures** in the story dropdown start in **I was there** and
   follow errands the druid picked up while travelling. **Personal memories**
   collects his childhood and early druid training.
3. Click **Rehearse next**. The reading pause depends on the line's length,
   punctuation, and whether it was an emote. At zero, the next button is ready.
   Use **Auto rehearsal** to let the private rehearsal advance on its own.
   It waits while you pause, type an interlude or open either dropdown.
4. After the Hyjal line introducing the orc, click **Pause** and hover over
   **The orc's name** to preview the answer. Click it to use the answer, or type
   your own interlude and choose **Say** or **Emote**. An interlude leaves the
   next story line in place and gives the audience time to read the response.
5. Click **Resume**, wait for the pause to finish, and send the next line.
   Closing the window holds your place and the remaining pause.
6. Switch to **Live chat** to use `/say` and `/emote` with nearby players.
   Click **Send next** or assign **Tell next Ember Tales line** in WoW's Keybindings
   under **Ember Tales**. Outdoor speech requires a player input per line.
   The next live line is bookmarked per story, point of view and character
   across reloads. **Start over** resets the selected version in the current mode.

## Finding a story and setting the pace

Type in **Find** to filter the story dropdown by title, region, or section.
Press Enter to select a single result or open the filtered list. **Save
favourite** marks the current story, and **Favourites only** narrows the same
list. Favourites are saved per character. **Pick for me** chooses another
story from the current filtered list, so it also works with your favourites.
It opens the selected story at its bookmark.

Use **Camp view** after choosing a story. It folds away search, favourites
and the story and voice pickers so the next line and performance controls
remain visible in a smaller window. **Library** restores the full view.
Opening **Read script** temporarily expands the window. The current line
and any written interlude stay in place when you switch views.

**Pace** cycles through **Natural**, **Brisk**, and **Leisurely**. Changing it
adjusts the current reading pause and the next lines, including a paused run.
Auto rehearsal waits while you type in the search field or open a menu.

## Round off or tell more

After the main story, you'll reach a natural stopping point. Use **Round off**
to queue one closing line or gesture, or **Tell more** for four extra lines
about what happened afterwards. Send the selected lines at the usual pace.
You can still answer a question or use your own interlude before choosing.
Auto rehearsal stops at the choice. Turn it back on if you want it to read
the ending. Live chat remembers both the ending you picked and your next line.

**Read script** opens a private view of the selected voice, both endings,
and the prepared replies. It marks the current next line and shows how
much time the reading pauses would take at your current pace. The estimate
excludes optional replies, interludes, and time waiting for a click.
Opening the script holds any running pause or auto rehearsal. Use **Back**
and **Resume** to continue a paused performance.

## Famous figures

The druid can tell seven stories about familiar Warcraft characters. **Read
script** shows the sources and a note on how he knows each account. The
first-person quest versions suit characters who made those journeys. In
the Illidan and Arthas stories, even the memory version recalls how the
druid learned the news and what he saw afterwards.

At the natural stopping point, **Ask the camp** offers an optional question
and pauses for answers. Hover over it to read the question first. **How do
you know?** is a separate prepared reply. When the audience is ready, pick
an ending and use **Resume**.

- *The Ring I Brought Tyrande* — Jer'kai's signet and Tyrande's request that the younger adventurers hear a tale. [Doling Justice](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=2972/doling-justice)
- *The Portrait Behind the Moons* — the family portrait returned to Tirion Fordring. [Of Love and Family](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=5848/of-love-and-family)
- *The Night Illidan Walked Free* — accounts of Illidan's release during the Third War. [Blizzard's Warcraft III recap](https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/23679744/burning-crusade-classic-the-story-so-far)
- *When the Prince Came Home* — civilians' and guards' accounts of Arthas's return. [Forsaken Kingdom recap](https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24302500/warcraft-iii-reforged-forsaken-kingdom-what-s-next-panel-recap)
- *The Road Both Camps Took* — accounts of how Jaina and Thrall's alliance reached the people carrying water on Hyjal. [Warcraft III timeline](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/story/timeline/chapter-4)
- *The Stone Magni Asked For* — Magni Bronzebeard and the memorial for Sully Balloo. [A King's Tribute](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=700/a-kings-tribute)
- *What Cairne Sent After the Horn* — a tauren hunter's account of Cairne's request, told in Moonglade with an interpreter. [Rites of the Earthmother](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=776/rites-of-the-earthmother)

## Warcraft I–III

Find this category in the story picker or search for `WC1`, `WC2`, or `WC3`.
The first two wars work as histories the druid learnt from survivors and
veterans. In the Third War, the barrow and Cenarius stories also offer
firsthand versions from the edge of those events. **Read script** explains
what he saw, what he heard, and where the narrative uses invented RP details.

- *What They Called Rexxar* — the same conflict remembered from Theramore and from Durotar. [Blizzard's Rexxar history](https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/11218304)
- *A Light in the Barrow* — an invented tale about helping the druids return during the Third War. [Warcraft III recap](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/23229617/warcraft-iii-the-story-so-far)
- *The Grove After Cenarius* — what the druid saw after Grom Hellscream and the Warsong killed Cenarius. [Blizzard's original WoW manual](https://us.media.blizzard.com/manuals/wow/wow-classic-manual-enUS.pdf)
- *A Name for the Next Boat* — a survivor describes the evacuation after Stormwind fell in the First War. [Warcraft I timeline](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/story/timeline/chapter-1)
- *The Banner at Blackrock* — a veteran remembers hearing of Lothar's death and Turalyon taking command. [Warcraft II timeline](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/story/timeline/chapter-2)
- *The Last Delivery to Karazhan* — a carter's family waits for word as rumours about Medivh and Khadgar spread. [Blizzard's Warcraft III recap](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/23229617/warcraft-iii-the-story-so-far)
- *Alleria and the Far Bank* — a Lordaeron veteran remembers one of the high elf rangers who came to help in the Second War. [Blizzard's Warcraft III recap](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/23229617/warcraft-iii-the-story-so-far)
- *Khadgar's Name in Stone* — a conversation by Stormwind's memorial about the expedition beyond the Dark Portal. [Beyond the Dark Portal](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/story/timeline/chapter-03) and [Blizzard's Stormwind guide](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/20142731/welcome-to-stormwind-a-guided-tour)

## Personal asides and chat hints

With **Personal asides: On**, some lines reveal a separate **Personal aside**
button. Hover to read the comment. Click it to say it, or keep telling the
story. An aside is available once per line. The toggle hides these buttons
whenever you want to stick to the main script.

**Chat hints** starts off. Turn it on in **Live chat** to match nearby
`/say` questions with replies to the line you just told. For example, after
Hyjal's reference to the orc, "What was the orc named?" suggests the line
about Gorak. A related question without an exact match may get a neutral
reply in the current point of view. **Use suggestion** puts the text into
an empty interlude draft for you to edit or send. The preview indicates
Darnassian when the prepared reply uses it.

The matcher runs locally while the window is open and a relevant reply is
available. It skips your own messages, unreadable languages and secret chat
values. It keeps no chat history, sends no automatic replies, and uses no
external AI service.

## Darnassian

The druid sometimes uses a word or phrase such as *shan'do* or *Ishnu-alah*
within his usual speech. Lines marked **[Darnassian]** use WoW's Darnassian chat
language when your character speaks it. Listeners who don't know the language
see WoW's obfuscated text. On other characters, the story uses their default
language. Some replies are themselves Darnassian asides.

**Next speech** cycles through **Script**, **Darnassian**, and **Default**.
The preview shows the language that will be used for the upcoming spoken
line. The setting returns to **Script** after that line is delivered.
Prepared replies and custom interludes use their own language.

To choose Darnassian for your own interlude, type the line and click
**Darnassian** next to **Say**. The button sends your wording as entered and
chooses the in-game language for that line. Use **Say** for a mixed-language
line everyone who understands your default language can read.

For ordinary chat, `/tale language darnassian` switches the game's spoken
language in the current and main chat boxes. Type your next `/say` as usual.
Use `/tale language default` to switch back, `/tale language common` to choose
Common explicitly if your character knows it, or `/tale language` to check
the current setting. The command selects the game's language and leaves your
wording as entered. Prepared story lines keep their own language choices.

### Campfire moments

- *A Light Along the Shore* is an original story about keeping a lantern lit for someone out on the water.

- *The Other Side of the Fire* is about sharing a camp with a stranger in the Barrens.
- *The Rain in the Tin Cup* is about a small lesson on a wet evening in the Wetlands.

### Personal memories

- *The Tree I Couldn't Hear* and *My Shan'do's Bad Knee* are original memories from his early years in Ashenvale.
- *The Bear Who Let Me Wait* draws on Dendrite Starblaze's [Great Bear Spirit](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=5929/great-bear-spirit) assignment in Moonglade. The lesson's dialogue and personal recollections are original RP.
- *The Cat Above the Door*, *The Patient Who Said No*, and *The Seed in My Boot* are original stories about his training, learning to listen as a healer, and family in Ashenvale.

The druid's conversations and personal anecdotes are original roleplay.
Gorak and Hargan are names used in the imagined Hyjal account.
The expressions are listed in the [Darnassian language reference](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Darnassian_(language)).
